PROFESSIONAL DISCIPLINE

Quality Design through a Player Experience lens.

Quality Design applies quality expertise within development, using player perspective, evidence, technical investigation and risk analysis to influence how products are designed, built and improved.

My practice concentrates on the boundary between technical quality and player reality: connecting intended outcomes with evidence of what players actually experience, then translating that learning back into actionable questions for Quality, Design, Engineering and player-facing disciplines.

Player-Centred Quality

Quality should operate on both sides of release. Shift Left challenges assumptions, intended outcomes and systemic risk earlier. Shift Right uses Live behaviour and player evidence to understand what actually happened.

Evidence & Insight

I combine different forms of player evidence to interrogate assumptions. Research, BI, Community, Customer Support, telemetry, engagement information and direct observation answer different questions.

The aim is not to replace specialist researchers or data scientists, but to connect their evidence with testing, technical context and player behaviour so development teams can act on it.

Evidence Synthesis Player Sentiment ADO Dashboards Telemetry Research / Insights Technical Translation

Technical & Adversarial Quality

I deliberately investigate how systems behave when players optimise, misuse, exploit or interact with them beyond the expected happy path.

Technical Risk Analysis Destructive Testing Exploratory Testing Regression Testing Cross-Play Testing Root Cause Analysis Log Analysis Exploits & Anti-Cheat

Capability Building

Specialist knowledge becomes more useful when other people can apply it independently. I turn exploratory and destructive approaches into reusable methods and use questioning and knowledge sharing to help other disciplines reason about quality and technical risk.
